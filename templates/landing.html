abbieysearch (shown as abbiey.search in the logo) is a privacy-first web search engine. We help you find information on the public web without using your queries to profile you for ads. You do not need an account to search — sign in is only for optional features like bookmarks, synced history, and developer tools.

Your searches stay yours. We do not log queries for advertising or resale. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.
