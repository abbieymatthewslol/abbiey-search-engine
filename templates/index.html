{# ===== PRIVACY ===== #}

Privacy

abbieysearch does not write your queries to server-side search logs. Optional history lives only where you turn it on (this device and, if you are signed in, your account row).

Incognito session

While incognito is on, recent queries are not saved locally or to your account, even if search history is enabled.

Precise location

Optional. When enabled, the browser can share your current location for nearby searches like food, taxis, delivery, and local businesses. Turning it off clears stored coordinates from this session.
