Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 April 2026  ·  Effective immediately

abbiey.search is a privacy-first search engine. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how. We keep it short because we genuinely collect very little.

1. Who We Are

abbiey.search is operated by Abbiey Matthews (abbiey.search, "we", "our"). You can reach us at privacy@abbieysearch.com.

2. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not log your search queries server-side.
  • We do not build a profile of your interests, searches, or behaviour.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers.
  • We do not use third-party advertising networks.
  • We do not set persistent third-party tracking cookies.

3. Data We Collect

3.1 Account Data (optional)

If you create an account, you provide us:

  • Email address — used for account recovery and optional email verification only.
  • Display name — used to personalise your dashboard.
  • Password hash — stored securely via Supabase Auth (bcrypt). We never store or see your plaintext password.

You can sign up with Google OAuth instead of email/password. In that case, we receive your Google email and name only; no other Google data is accessed.

3.2 Usage Data

Registered users may optionally store bookmarks and search history in their account. This data is yours and visible only to you. You can delete it at any time from your profile page.

3.3 Technical / Server Logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically records standard web server logs (IP address, HTTP method, URL path, response code, timestamp) for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention. We do not analyse these logs for marketing purposes. Search query text is not present in these logs.

3.4 Public signals (OSINT)

Optional Public signals lookups run only when you choose them (for example, expanding “Public signals (OSINT)” on a detected domain, IP, or email, or using the image lightbox action for the page host). They are not part of normal web search and are not written to your search history.

When you use this feature, our servers query public technical sources on your behalf, currently including:

  • DNS-over-HTTPS (Cloudflare resolver at cloudflare-dns.com) for DNS records such as A, AAAA, MX, NS, and TXT.
  • RDAP via rdap.org for registration metadata for domains and IPv4 addresses.
  • Reverse DNS (PTR) via the platform resolver for IPv4 addresses.
  • TLS certificate metadata (optional module) from a standard TLS handshake to port 443, parsed with our application libraries.
  • Local dig / whois (optional self-hosted module) only when those programs are installed on the server and enabled in configuration—typical for developer setups on Linux/Kali, not used on our default production image.

Those operators may process the hostname or IP you looked up according to their own policies. We apply short-lived in-memory caching on our side to reduce repeat queries; we do not store OSINT result payloads in your account or in analytics tables.

Self-hosters can disable or narrow OSINT using environment variables documented in .env.example (ABBIEY_OSINT_ENABLED, ABBIEY_OSINT_MODULES).

3.5 Cookies

We set the minimum cookies required to operate the service:

  • session — a signed, server-side session cookie that keeps you logged in. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. Expires when your browser session ends (or 7 days if you tick "remember me").
  • search_unlock — remembers whether you have unlocked unlimited search on this device. No personal data inside.

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Google Analytics is configured in "Basic" mode and only fires if you have not opted out via browser settings or an ad blocker.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following sub-processors. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — database and authentication. Data hosted in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Vercel (vercel.com) — web hosting and edge delivery. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Stripe (stripe.com) — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (google.com/analytics) — aggregate, anonymised usage statistics. IP anonymisation enabled. Google Privacy Policy
  • DuckDuckGo (duckduckgo.com) — search results are proxied through our servers. Your IP is not sent to DuckDuckGo.
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com) — DNS-over-HTTPS resolver when you explicitly use Public signals (OSINT).
  • RDAP / registry operators — when you use OSINT, we follow redirects from rdap.org to the relevant registry or RIR; those operators process the domain or IP being looked up.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account.
  • Bookmarks and history: deleted immediately when you remove them, or when you delete your account.
  • Server logs: 30 days, then automatically purged by Vercel.

To delete your account and all associated data, go to Profile → Delete Account or email privacy@abbieysearch.com.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Email privacy@abbieysearch.com to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days.

8. Children

abbiey.search is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Database access requires authenticated, encrypted connections. We apply Content Security Policy, HSTS, and other hardening headers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via the email address on your account. The "Last updated" date at the top will always reflect the current version.
