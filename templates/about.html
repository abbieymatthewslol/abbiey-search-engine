Search for people who don’t stop at the first link

I built abbieysearch for investigators, truth-seekers, and anyone who cross-checks before they trust. You can use it without an account. Sign in only if you want bookmarks, synced history, or developer tools.

I’m aiming for direct answers, visible evidence, and honest tradeoffs — assumptions, edge cases, useful next steps, and the strongest counterpoint when the answer is messy. Parts of it are still rough; that’s me learning in public.

Tools for repeat investigations

Built for people who run the same query twice with different depth — entity detection, optional public OSINT, indexed .onion references, breach check, and adversarial answer modes.

  • Entity bar: IP, domain, email, username, crypto, coordinates, and more — one search, structured context.
  • Public signals (OSINT): expand-on-demand RDAP, DNS, and related public metadata — lawful recon only (how we handle OSINT).
  • Onion / Tor tab: clearnet-indexed .onion references via Ahmia + DDG — not a dark-web crawl (scope & limits).
  • Breach check: private email exposure lookup — no storage, no query logs.
  • People finder: optional public-web hints before a focused people search.
  • Research Assistant: follow-ups grounded in your results — save locally or to your account.

How much depth you want

Sometimes you want a fast answer. Sometimes you want the raw material, competing viewpoints, and receipts. You can move between both on the same search — no switching tools.

Different questions need different kinds of answers. I put depth, evidence, and tone on the results page instead of hiding everything behind one default response.

  • Depth: Quick, Standard, Deep, or Research.
  • Evidence: Links only, key quotes, or fuller excerpts.
  • Answer style: Direct, balanced, or adversarial.

Research Assistant

On any results page, open the Research panel for follow-ups grounded in what you already found. Attach images for visual questions. Save chats locally, or to your account when signed in, and pick up later.

Privacy should not be a premium feature

No third-party ad trackers. No silent profiling. No server-side query logging tied to your searches. You can search without an account — sign in only if you want bookmarks, synced history, saved research chats, or API access.
